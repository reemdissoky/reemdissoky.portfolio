What are these notes about?

They are short, practical notes on the marketing problems I keep meeting in Saudi Arabia, Egypt and the wider region.

Each one starts from real work. An exhibition in Riyadh. A resort with a conversion problem. A B2B company that needed AI assistants to name it correctly. The notes keep the method and leave out the noise.

They are written for business owners, marketing leads and hiring managers who have to make a decision, not for other marketers.

Why write them down?

Because most marketing mistakes are expensive and repeatable.

Spending on ads when the problem is in the sales call. Translating copy instead of writing it. Hiring a team before anyone owns the plan. Treating booth traffic as leads. They come up again and again, and the fix is usually simpler than the mistake.

Writing it down makes me say it plainly. If a point can't survive a short note, it probably isn't worth a long one.

How are the notes written?

Every note answers a question someone actually asks, with the direct answer first.

  • The answer up front. The first lines under each heading say what to do. The rest explains why.
  • Steps you can act on. Numbered where order matters, so you can start with the first one.
  • Real numbers only. Where a result is quoted, it comes from my own work, like 600+ booth registrations at FFCC KSA 2026, or a conversion rate taken from 3% to 20% at Goot Resorts.
  • Both markets in mind. Saudi Arabia and Egypt often read in two languages, and the notes say so where it changes the advice.

What I write about

The same topics I work on. Each one links to the service behind it.

  • GEO: getting named correctly by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.
  • SEO: the foundations that still decide whether you are found on Google.
  • Employer branding: one real reason to work at your company, said the same way everywhere.
  • Creative campaign ideas: concepts built on an audience thesis, including exhibitions and events.
  • Social media: a strategy per platform, organic and paid.
  • Copywriting: English and Arabic, written for the reader, not translated for them.
  • Brand communication: one point of view across every channel.

Where should you start?

Start with the note closest to the decision in front of you.

Choosing who to hire for marketing: freelance marketer or agency. Planning a booth: exhibition leads, not footfall. Worried about AI search: GEO vs SEO. Struggling to hire: employer branding for small companies.

If you want to see how the advice holds up in practice, the work is here, and this is how it runs.

New notes go up when the work teaches me something worth passing on.
