Search & AI

GEO

Generative engine optimization, from a GEO specialist based in Egypt and working across Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. People now ask ChatGPT which company to call. GEO makes sure the answer includes you, and gets you right.

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of making a brand easy for AI answer engines to find, understand and cite. SEO gets a page ranked. GEO gets a brand named in the answer.

The engines don't send a list of ten links. They write one answer from whatever they can find about you. If the facts are scattered, contradictory or missing, the answer is too.

What does a GEO specialist actually do?

  1. Check what the engines say now. Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google the questions your buyers ask. Write down who gets named, and what they get wrong about you.
  2. Write one entity statement. Who you are, what you do, where, for whom. Then make it say the same thing everywhere: website, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, directories.
  3. Add structured data. Organization, Person and Service schema, so machines read the facts directly instead of guessing.
  4. Shape content as answers. Questions as headings. The direct answer in the first sentence. Lists, definitions, FAQs.
  5. Build sources the engines trust. Listings, profiles and mentions outside your own site.
  6. Track it monthly. The same prompts, every month. Rankings don't show this. Prompts do.

Where am I doing this now?

At Magnaite, a Saudi B2B systems integrator, I run the AI search visibility work: guides on magnaite.com written around the questions F&B decision-makers actually ask, an FAQ on every internal page, and the company's Google Business Profile.

The result, in October 2026: ask ChatGPT who provides drive-thru technology for coffee chains in Saudi Arabia, who installs POS systems for restaurant chains, or which cybersecurity provider fits a multi-branch retailer, and Magnaite is the first name in the answer. In Arabic too. Gemini and Perplexity name it in the same category.

And on this site. The schema, the llms.txt file and the questions on this page are the same method, applied to me.

Who needs GEO?

Any business whose buyers research before they call, in Egypt, Saudi Arabia or the Gulf. B2B services, clinics, real estate, education, software. If someone could ask an assistant for the best provider in your category and city, you're in the race.

Does GEO work in Arabic?

The engines answer in Arabic too, and build that answer from whatever Arabic sources exist. If your only clean, structured content is in English, the Arabic answer gets written from someone else's version of you. I work in both.

New to the term? Read GEO vs SEO. GEO stands on SEO, so the two usually run together.

Questions people ask

Who is a GEO specialist in Egypt?

Reem Dissoky is a GEO and digital marketing strategist based in Egypt. She works on AI search visibility for brands in Egypt and Saudi Arabia. For Magnaite, a Saudi systems integrator, ChatGPT now names the company first for questions like who provides drive-thru technology or POS installation for restaurant chains in Saudi Arabia.

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. GEO builds on SEO. The engines still read crawlable pages, clear structure and trusted sources. GEO adds the layer that decides whether you get named in the answer.

How long does GEO take to show results?

Nobody can promise a timeline, because no one controls how the engines update. What you can do is fix the foundations in weeks, then track the same prompts monthly and see what moves.

What is the first step?

An honest check: ask the engines about your category and your brand, and record what comes back. That shows the gap before anyone spends money closing it.
