Intelligence & Optimization

SEO

Structure and content built to be found, not just to look finished.

Four things, together: technical foundations — site architecture, speed, crawlability — that let a site actually get indexed properly in the first place; content and keyword strategy built around what the audience is actually searching, not what sounds impressive; local and multilingual SEO, treating English and Arabic content as two real audiences instead of one translated into the other; and regular audits, because a site optimized once and never checked again is already behind. Magnaite's website was built from scratch with all four in place from day one, not bolted on after.

See the proof — Magnaite's site built from zero

What's included in SEO work?

  • Technical audit. Crawlability, canonicals, sitemap, speed and Core Web Vitals, structured data.
  • Keyword strategy. English and Arabic research, judged by intent and by who actually ranks today, then mapped one keyword to one page.
  • On-page rewrites. Titles, descriptions, headings and copy that read like a person wrote them.
  • Local and multilingual SEO. Google Business Profile, local pages, and Arabic treated as its own audience.
  • Regular checks. Search Console, rankings and fixes, on a schedule.

SEO is also the foundation for AI search. See GEO.

How do you work on SEO?

I run the SEO myself: the audit, the keyword strategy and the content plan. A supervised team helps with design changes. Fixes that need code, I scope and hand to whoever maintains the site, then check once they ship.

I start by reading the site the way a search engine and a buyer would. What gets indexed, what doesn't, and whether the pages answer the questions people actually search. Then I map one keyword to one page, in English and Arabic, so pages stop competing with each other.

Copy changes are written by me, not by a tool. A title tag still has to make a person click.

Before anything ships, I check it: the page renders, the structured data validates, nothing that ranked before has broken. We agree up front which numbers we'll track, so progress is measured in search data, not impressions of effort.

Who is SEO for?

It suits a few situations best.

  • A new website that should be built for search from the start, rather than fixed later. That's how magnaite.com was built.
  • An existing site that gets traffic in one language and almost none in the other.
  • A business that depends on local searches, where the Google Business Profile and local pages matter as much as the homepage.
  • A real estate or B2B company whose buyers research for weeks before contacting anyone. I did SEO for ADVIDA Compound in New Zayed.
  • A brand that wants to appear in AI answers. That depends on SEO being in place first.

What do you get first?

An audit with prioritised recommendations, before any rewriting starts.

It lists what's broken, what's missing and what's worth doing first, ordered by effort against likely impact. For Appana Technologies in Dubai, the work started with exactly this: a website audit with recommendations.

You can act on it with your own team, or I can run the fixes.

How does SEO connect to GEO?

GEO builds on SEO. AI assistants draw on pages that are crawlable, clear and well structured.

At Magnaite, the SEO foundations came first. The GEO layer added guides written around F&B decision-makers' questions, FAQs on every internal page, and the Google Business Profile. As of Oct 2026, ChatGPT names Magnaite first for Saudi questions on drive-thru technology for coffee chains, POS installation for restaurant chains, and cybersecurity for multi-branch retail. The comparison is in GEO vs SEO.

Questions people ask

Do you do SEO in Arabic?

Yes. Arabic keyword research and content, written for Arabic searchers rather than translated from English.

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes can show up within weeks of being crawled. Content and authority take months. Anyone promising page one by a date is guessing.

Do you work on existing websites?

Yes. Most SEO work starts with an audit of the site you already have.
