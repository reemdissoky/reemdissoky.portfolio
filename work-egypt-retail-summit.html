Brand Copywriting, Website & Social · Nile Ritz, Egypt

Egypt Retail Summit

Annual summit created by BiznEX Holding and retail experts, bringing key retail market players together to build relationships and share knowledge.

What is the Egypt Retail Summit?

It is an annual summit created by BiznEX Holding together with retail experts. Its purpose is to bring key players in Egypt's retail market into one room to build relationships and share knowledge.

The edition I worked on took place at the Nile Ritz-Carlton in Cairo on 23 March 2021.

What did I work on?

Three pieces of the same voice: the brand creative writing, the website copy, and the social media.

  • Brand creative writing. The core language the summit used to describe itself.
  • Website copy. The pages people read before deciding to attend.
  • Social media. The content that carried the summit to its audience before and around the event.

Writing all three let one voice run across every place someone met the summit.

Why does a summit need its own brand voice?

Because it competes for the time of busy people.

Retail leaders are invited to a lot of events. A summit has to tell them, quickly, why this one is worth a day away from the business. That is a copywriting problem before it is a design problem.

A clear voice also helps the people on stage and in the room. They know what kind of event they are walking into.

What was the context in early 2021?

Retail in Egypt, like everywhere, was coming out of a hard 2020. Stores had faced closures and changing customer habits. Many businesses were cautious about what came next.

A summit built around relationships and shared knowledge fits that moment. When a market is uncertain, people want to hear how others in the same position are thinking. The copy had to speak to that, without overpromising.

How does this connect to BiznEX?

The Egypt Retail Summit is a BiznEX Holding creation, and it is one of several BiznEX projects I worked on.

That included the brand rebuild and the "Find Your Business Match" campaign for BiznEX, and the script and voiceover direction for the BiznEX Holding TV ad.

Who reads summit copy, and what do they need?

Three groups read it, and each needs something different.

  • Attendees need a reason to give up a working day, and a clear idea of who else will be there.
  • Speakers and partners need to see that the event is serious enough to put their name on.
  • The wider market follows on social, and needs to understand what the summit stands for even if they never attend.

Good event copy serves all three without splitting into three voices. That is why the brand writing comes first and everything else is built from it.

How do I approach event copy now?

I start from the reader's question: why should I be in that room?

Everything else follows from the answer. The website explains it. Social repeats it in smaller pieces. The brand line compresses it into something people can remember.

Who is this kind of work for?

Organisers of summits, expos and conferences who need the event to sound like itself everywhere it appears.

That usually means one person writing the brand line, the website and the social content, so nothing gets lost between them. I also work on the ground at events, so the copy is written by someone who knows what the room will actually feel like.

The words come first. The rest follows.
