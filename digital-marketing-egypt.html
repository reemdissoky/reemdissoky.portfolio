Egypt

Digital marketing strategy in Egypt, from someone who works here.

I'm based in Egypt. I've built campaigns in Cairo for an investment expo, a retail summit, real estate and new brands, in Arabic and English, for the people who actually read them.

What do I do for businesses in Egypt?

I build the marketing strategy, then run it: positioning, campaigns, content, search and AI search, with one person accountable for the plan.

  • Strategy and positioning. What you say, to whom, and why they should believe it. One point of view across every channel.
  • Campaigns and events. Concept through execution, on-ground and digital.
  • Copywriting in Arabic and English. Written in the language the audience reads in, not translated into it.
  • SEO and GEO. Getting found on Google, and named correctly by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. More on GEO.
  • Social media. A strategy per platform, not one post resized five ways.

Why do Egyptian brands need two voices that sound like one?

A business in Egypt usually speaks two languages at once. English on the website and the pitch deck. Egyptian Arabic in the comments, the WhatsApp replies, the sales call.

If those two voices don't sound like the same company, the customer notices before you do. Most of my copy work starts there.

How does a project start?

It starts with the goal, not the channels.

Send me the business number you need to move and what you've tried so far. From there, I ask the questions that shape the plan: who buys, what they compare you with, what slows them down, and which language they read in.

Then we agree the KPIs together, before any work starts. That way we both know what success looks like, and I can be held to it.

What happens once the plan is agreed?

I build the strategy myself and run my own specialties hands-on: concepts, copy in Arabic and English, SEO and GEO, on-ground event work and video.

Where the work needs more hands, a supporting team works under my supervision, mainly on design, media buying and video editing. Everything passes a quality check with me before it goes live.

I report against the KPIs we agreed, not against activity. If a number isn't moving, we look at why, including the parts after the lead, like the sales conversation or the offer itself.

One plan. One person answering for it.
