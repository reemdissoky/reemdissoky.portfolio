How I work

One person accountable.
A team behind the plan.

I build the strategy myself. I run my own specialties hands-on. A supporting team works under my supervision. You deal with one person, and that person answers for the numbers.

What does working with me look like?

You get one accountable marketing lead, not an agency. Think of it as a fractional marketing manager: senior enough to own the strategy, close enough to the work to do it.

Three layers. Each one has a clear owner.

1. The strategy is mine.

I decide which bets are worth making, which channels earn budget, and what we measure. It gets written down, so you can read it, question it and hold me to it.

2. My specialties, hands-on.

Strategy, concepts, copy in English and Arabic, SEO and GEO, and the on-the-ground work at events. I don't brief these out. I do them.

3. A supporting team, supervised.

When a brief needs more hands than I have, a supporting team works under my supervision, mainly on design, media buying and video editing. They work from my brief. Their work comes back through me.

How do you keep the plan on track?

With KPIs agreed before anything ships, and a quality check on everything that does.

  • KPIs first. We agree on the numbers that matter to the business: leads, bookings, applications, signed deals. Followers and likes can be signals. They're not the goal.
  • One brief, one standard. Every piece of work is checked against the brief and the brand before it goes live: copy, design, ads, landing pages.
  • Reporting you can read. What moved, what didn't, and what we change next.
  • Diagnosis past the dashboard. When a number stalls, I follow it wherever it goes. At Goot Resorts that meant sales calls and a site visit in Riyadh.

Who I work with

Any industry

B2B tech and systems integration, hospitality, real estate, healthcare, HR tech, events and expos, F&B, retail. The method holds. The audience changes.

Any size

From a brand with no channels yet to an established group running an expo. What matters is that someone owns the plan, and answers for it.

Why one strategist instead of an agency?

An agency gives you an account manager who relays the brief. With me, the person who wrote the strategy is the person checking the work. Nothing gets lost in the handover, because there isn't one.

The trade-off is real: there's one of me, so capacity is limited. If you need forty campaigns a month across ten markets, hire the agency.

Questions people ask

Is this a freelancer or an agency?

Neither, exactly. I work independently and take responsibility for the strategy and the results, like a fractional marketing manager. When the work needs more hands, a supporting team works under my supervision. You get one point of contact either way.

Which markets and languages do you cover?

Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Egypt and Kuwait. I write and work in English and Arabic.

Do you work remotely?

Remote by default, from Egypt. On-site when the work needs it: I ran Magnaite's booth at FFCC KSA 2026 in Riyadh in person, for the full event.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. Send me the goal, the timeline and what you've already tried, and I'll come back with a plan and a number.

What do you need from me to start?

The business goal in one sentence, access to whatever data exists (ads accounts, CRM, analytics), and one person on your side who can make decisions.

Have a brief? Start with the goal.
