Strategy

Brand Communication

Positioning and messaging that gives a brand a consistent point of view across every channel it shows up in — from a campaign concept down to a single caption.

I don't start with channels. I start with the position — the one sentence that has to be true before anything else makes sense. BiznEX needed to move from "an expo with almost no brand history" to "the recovery every entrepreneur was owed" — that shift is what let a tomorrow-facing slogan, three named zones, and a full visual identity all agree with each other instead of reading as separate decisions. Magnaite needed a different kind of consistency: a systems-integration brand that has to read as trustworthy in a two-minute booth conversation and in a LinkedIn caption, using the same handful of words each time.
