Real Estate & Interior Finishing · Saudi Arabia · Project-based

EMAC

Social media production and management for EMAC — interior finishing and real estate services across Riyadh, Dammam, Jeddah, and Al-Ahsa. Content spanning material and color education, interior lifestyle content, and National Day-tied promotions.

emac.sa ↗

What did EMAC need from social media?

EMAC needed social media that worked across four cities and three kinds of content without losing a clear identity.

EMAC provides interior finishing and real estate services in Riyadh, Dammam, Jeddah and Al-Ahsa. That is a wide footprint for one brand voice. The audience includes homeowners, buyers and people planning a renovation, each at a different stage of a big decision.

The work was project-based. I handled social media production and management for the account.

Why teach materials and colour on social?

Because people buying interior finishing are making choices they will live with for years, and most of them are not experts.

Someone choosing finishes for a home usually starts with a feeling: warm, bright, calm, modern. They rarely start with material names or colour codes. Education content bridges that gap. It gives the audience the words and the confidence to make a decision.

It also positions the brand well. A company that explains its craft openly is easier to trust than one that only shows finished rooms.

So material and colour education became one of the core content streams.

What role did lifestyle content play?

Lifestyle content showed what the finished space feels like to live in.

Education answers "what should I choose". Lifestyle answers "why does it matter". Interior content works best when the two sit side by side. One builds understanding, the other builds desire.

I wanted the feed to move between both, so a follower could learn something and picture their own home in the same scroll.

How did National Day fit in?

Saudi National Day is one of the biggest moments in the Saudi calendar, and brands are expected to show up for it.

For EMAC, National Day-tied promotions connected the brand to a shared occasion while giving the audience a reason to act. The challenge with any national occasion is standing out among everyone else posting on the same day. That comes down to fitting the moment to what the brand actually does, not just adding a flag to a standard post.

How do I run social media for a multi-city brand?

I set the content pillars first, then plan the calendar around them.

For EMAC the pillars were clear: education, lifestyle and seasonal promotion. Once those are fixed, every post has a job. The calendar stays balanced, and the brand sounds the same in every city it serves.

I build the strategy and write the copy in Arabic and English. On accounts like this, design and media buying are handled by a supporting team under my supervision. Before anything ships, it goes through a quality check against the plan and the KPIs agreed at the start.

You can read more about how I run accounts on the social media services page, and about the Saudi market on digital marketing in Saudi Arabia.

Every post should have a reason to exist.

The same structure works for any brand that serves several cities from one account.
