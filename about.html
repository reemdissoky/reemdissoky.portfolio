What do I believe about marketing?

Marketing should move a business number, and one person should answer for it.

So I agree the KPIs before the work starts. I check everything before it ships.

The audience comes first. Who is buying, what slows them down, what they read and in which language. The idea follows from that.

The job doesn't end at the lead, either. At Goot Resorts, conversion was stuck at 3%. Testing ads didn't move it. Fixing how sales handled the leads did, and it reached 20% in six months.

Find where the number is stuck. Fix that first.

What languages and markets do I work in?

I work in English and Arabic, across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Kuwait.

I write both languages myself, matched to the platform and the reader. For Magnaite, English case-study content ran on LinkedIn and Instagram, and Arabic-first content ran on X and TikTok. In Egypt, the right register is often Egyptian Arabic, because that is how the customer talks.
