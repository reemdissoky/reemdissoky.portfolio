Questions people ask

Who is Reem Dissoky?

I'm a digital marketing strategist based in Egypt, with seven years across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Kuwait. I work across industries and project sizes, in English and Arabic. You'll also see my name spelled Reem Dessoky, Desoky or Desoki.

What do you do?

I build the marketing strategy, then make sure it gets built right: positioning, campaigns, content, SEO and GEO, exhibitions, employer branding. I run my specialties hands-on and supervise a supporting team for the rest.

How is that different from an agency?

You get one accountable person instead of an account manager relaying briefs. The person who wrote the strategy checks the work and answers for the KPIs. How I work.

Where do you work?

Remotely from Egypt, and in person when the work needs it. Clients in Saudi Arabia, Egypt, the UAE and Kuwait.

Do you do GEO?

Yes. I help brands get named, and described correctly, by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. More on GEO.
